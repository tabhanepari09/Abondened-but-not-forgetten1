THE BIG IDEA

From leftover hardware
to a listening network.

Old landers cannot simply be switched back on. Many were never designed to last for decades. But their locations, surviving structures and mission records can guide new, low-power instruments. A future network could let several sites observe the same event and help scientists tell whether a signal came from the ground, the Sun or distant space.

01 / INSPECT

Find what survived

Map the site and check for damage from temperature swings, dust, radiation and time.

02 / ADD

Bring new sensors

Install independent instruments designed for the harsh environment rather than assuming old electronics still work.

03 / CONNECT

Share observations

Use a reliable relay to send measurements to Earth and compare readings from different locations.

04 / LEARN

Build a longer record

Repeated measurements reveal slow changes that a short landing mission could never see.

This page presents a research concept, not a funded or approved mission plan. The radar animation is illustrative; it is not connected to a real lunar sensor or live telemetry.
